CLASS IX · DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION

Lesson 4: Decimal Number System to Other Number Systems

Learn how to convert decimal numbers into binary, octal and hexadecimal.

1. Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:

  • Explain the decimal number system.
  • Convert decimal numbers to binary.
  • Convert decimal numbers to octal.
  • Convert decimal numbers to hexadecimal.

2. What Is the Decimal Number System?

The decimal number system is a number system with a base of 10. It uses ten digits, from 0 to 9. It is the number system we commonly use in everyday life.

Base = 10 Digits = 0–9

Examples of Decimal Numbers

25₁₀, 147₁₀ and 202₁₀

Where Do We Use Decimal Numbers?

  • Counting money
  • Measuring distance
  • Calculating marks
  • Counting objects in everyday life

3. Converting Decimal to Binary

To convert a decimal number to binary, repeatedly divide the number by 2 and record each remainder.

  1. Divide the decimal number by 2.
  2. Record the remainder (0 or 1).
  3. Divide the quotient by 2 again.
  4. Continue until the quotient becomes 0.
  5. Read the remainders from bottom to top.

Worked Example: Convert 13₁₀ to Binary

Division Quotient Remainder
13 ÷ 261
6 ÷ 230
3 ÷ 211
1 ÷ 201
Read the remainders from bottom to top: 1101.

Therefore, 13₁₀ = 1101₂

4. Converting Decimal to Octal

To convert decimal to octal, follow the same repeated-division method, but divide by 8 instead of 2.

  1. Divide the decimal number by 8.
  2. Record the remainder.
  3. Continue dividing the quotient by 8 until it becomes 0.
  4. Read the remainders from bottom to top.

Worked Example: Convert 83₁₀ to Octal

Division Quotient Remainder
83 ÷ 8103
10 ÷ 812
1 ÷ 801
Read the remainders from bottom to top: 123.

Therefore, 83₁₀ = 123₈

5. Converting Decimal to Hexadecimal

To convert a decimal number to hexadecimal, repeatedly divide the number by 16. Record the remainders and read them from bottom to top.

Remember: Hexadecimal remainders from 10 to 15 are represented by the letters A to F.

Hexadecimal Reference Table

Decimal Value Hexadecimal Symbol
10A
11B
12C
13D
14E
15F

Worked Example: Convert 254₁₀ to Hexadecimal

Division Quotient Remainder
254 ÷ 161514
15 ÷ 16015

Step 1: Convert the remainders into hexadecimal symbols.

14 = E

15 = F

Step 2: Read the symbols from bottom to top.

Therefore, 254₁₀ = FE₁₆

8. Lesson Summary

  • The decimal number system has base 10 and uses digits 0–9.
  • To convert decimal to binary, repeatedly divide by 2.
  • To convert decimal to octal, repeatedly divide by 8.
  • To convert decimal to hexadecimal, repeatedly divide by 16.
  • For each method, read the remainders from bottom to top.
  • In hexadecimal, A–F represent decimal values 10–15.

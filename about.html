MEET THE TEAM

About DTI Learning Hub

We are a group of five students developing an educational website about Digital Number Systems for Class IX Digital Technology and Innovation (DTI).

Our goal is to make learning number systems more accessible through lessons, videos, practice activities and teaching resources.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a clear, organised and engaging learning space where students can understand decimal, binary, octal and hexadecimal number systems and practise converting numbers between them.

What Our Website Offers

  • Five organised lessons about digital number systems.
  • Student and teacher learning resources.
  • Educational videos and interactive quizzes.
  • Practice questions and real-life applications.
